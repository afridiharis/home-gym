# The FORGE Plan — Haris's L5-Safe Strength & Rehab Program

**Equipment:** Sportsroyals Power Tower (multi-grip pull-up bar, dip handles, vertical knee-raise station with back pad, push-up handles at the base).
**Goals, in order:** 1) rehabilitate and protect the L5 back, 2) get very strong, 3) visible abs, 4) stay consistent.

> ⚠️ This is a training plan, not medical advice. The "L5 issue" is self-reported and unconfirmed — this plan assumes the most common pattern (flexion-intolerant discogenic pain). If your symptoms are worse when standing/arching and better sitting, the rules partially invert: get assessed before following this. If symptoms persist beyond 6 weeks of sensible training, see a physio.

---

## The one idea everything follows from

Lumbar discs get injured by **bending the lower spine under load** — especially repeatedly, especially with rotation, especially in the first hour after waking (overnight disc hydration triples flexion stress). So:

- The spine stays **neutral and braced**; movement happens at the **hips**.
- Core training is ***anti*-movement**: planks, side planks, bird dogs, dead bugs, carries. This is also exactly how you build a six-pack — no crunches required, ever.
- **No kipping, no swinging, no dropping off the bar** (also: a freestanding tower can walk/tip under dynamic swings — put it on a rubber mat, and never anchor your feet under the frame).
- **Never train in the first hour after waking.** Two to three hours after is ideal.

## The traffic-light rules (these override everything)

| Light | Meaning | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 GREEN | Feeling fine, or symptoms retreating toward the spine | Train as planned, progress when targets are hit |
| 🟡 YELLOW | Niggly | The app trims volume ~40%. Move well, don't push |
| 🔴 RED | Rough day, or ANY pain/tingling running down a leg | Spine Reset + walk only. Leg symptoms are never trained through |

Judge yesterday's session by **this morning's back**. Worse morning = yesterday was too much.

**See a doctor the same day if:** numbness in the groin/saddle area, new bladder or bowel trouble, weakness in both legs or worsening weakness, a worsening dragging foot, fever/unexplained weight loss/severe night pain.

## Daily non-negotiables (~15 min total)

1. **Spine Reset (10 min, every single day)** — 5–6 slow cat-camels, then McGill's Big 3 as descending pyramids of 8–10s holds: curl-up 5-3-1, side plank holds both sides, bird dog 5-3-2. Progress by adding *reps*, never longer holds. Optional 20–40s dead hang to finish (skip if it makes leg symptoms worse; step down, never drop).
2. **Walk** — build to 30 min/day, 5+ days a week. Brisk, relaxed. Three 10-minute bouts count. This has the strongest evidence of anything in this document (WalkBack RCT, Lancet 2024: roughly doubled time to recurrence).
3. **Sitting hygiene** — break every 20–30 min, lumbar support, and hip-hinge (not spine-bend) to pick anything up. Practise the broomstick hinge drill until it's automatic.

## The week (4 training days)

Days rotate through four sessions; every session starts with the built-in warm-up (2-min march → cat-camel → mini Big 3 → ramp set):

| Session | Focus | Core lifts |
|---|---|---|
| **Upper A** | Pull-biased strength | Pull-up ladder ×4, dips ×3, rows under dip handles ×3, VKR knee raises, dead-hang finisher |
| **Lower A** | Legs + trunk | Squat ladder, hinge ladder, sliding leg curls (Ph3+), calf raises, hard-style plank, side plank |
| **Upper B** | Push-biased + volume pull | Pike push-up/handstand ladder ×4, push-up ladder, pull-ups (easier volume), L-sit work, scap pulls |
| **Lower B** | Legs + trunk | Squats (lighter), hinges, single-leg RDL patterning, calves, dead bugs, suitcase carries |

Rest 2–3 min after main lifts, 60–90s after accessories. Every pattern lands ~2×/week.

## Progression: ladders, not luck

Each movement pattern is a ladder of variations. Work in the rep window; when you hit the top of the window on all sets, climb one rung and drop to the bottom of the next window. Examples:

- **Vertical pull:** dead hang → scap pulls → negatives → assisted → chin-ups → pull-ups → chest-to-bar → archer → weighted (backpack, +2.5 kg jumps)
- **Dip:** support hold 60s → negatives → partials → full dips → slow tempo → weighted
- **Push:** incline → floor handles → decline → diamond → archer → pseudo-planche; separately pike push-ups → elevated pike → wall handstand → HSPU
- **Core:** VKR bent-knee raises → knees-to-chest → straight-leg → hanging knee raises (Ph3) → hanging leg raises (Ph4, optional); L-sit foot-supported → tuck → one-leg → full
- **Legs:** squats → split squats → Bulgarians (rear foot on the tower step; becomes your main leg lift with a hugged backpack) → step-ups → pistol *only if the back stays quiet*
- **Hinge:** glute bridge → single-leg bridge → hip thrust → single-leg hip thrust (+backpack)

## Phases (gated by symptoms, not the calendar)

1. **Calm the Fire** — irritable back. Spine Reset + walking + gentle groundwork only. *Gate: 7+ straight days of green/yellow mornings, zero leg symptoms.*
2. **Foundation** — full split at conservative rungs. Back-supported knee raises only; box-depth squats. *Gate: 3+ weeks consistent, green mornings, symptoms centralised or gone.*
3. **Build** — hanging knee raises, Bulgarians, tuck L-sits, bent-knee hollow holds unlock. Optional grease-the-groove pull-ups (4–6 easy sub-max singles/doubles spread through the day, never near failure). *Gate: 4–8 weeks fully symptom-free, mornings included.*
4. **Advance** — weighted pull-ups/dips, archer work, HSPU negatives, full L-sit. Hanging leg raises are optional, low-volume, never in the morning, dropped instantly on any symptom.

A flare resets you a phase. That's the system working, not failing.

## Realistic 12-month targets

10 strict pull-ups · 15 dips · 3×12 feet-elevated rows · 30 push-ups · 10s full L-sit · weighted Bulgarians +20 kg · visible abs (if the kitchen cooperates). First strict pull-up typically lands 4–12 weeks in; first dip 4–8 weeks.

## The abs equation

Abs become visible around **10–14% body fat** — that part is diet:

- **Deficit:** 300–500 kcal/day → lose 0.5–1% of bodyweight per week. Faster eats your strength.
- **Protein:** ~1.8–2.2 g per kg bodyweight daily (the app computes your numbers).
- Weigh daily, judge weekly averages. Diet break at maintenance every 6–8 weeks. If pull-up numbers drop two weeks running, eat a little more.
- Only broadly-supported extras: creatine 3–5 g/day, vitamin D. Sleep 7–9h — it's a training variable.

## Consistency system (the actual hard part)

- **The streak counts *any* session** — Spine Reset, a walk, a 5-minute Rescue circuit. Showing up is the metric.
- **Streak freezes** are earned (1 per 7 days trained, 2 banked max) and applied silently to missed days; a 48h **repair window** lets one slip be bridged by training today.
- **Implementation intention:** "On [day] at [time], right after [habit], I do [session]" — set in the app, mirrored in your Calendar with real alerts (Setup → Calendar buttons).
- Bad day? **5-Minute Rescue.** It fully counts. The only failure mode is zero.
